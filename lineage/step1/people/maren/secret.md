Piet is not Hal's son. He is Tobias Crane's.

It was one night, the autumn before I married Hal, in the mill loft. I never told Tobias and I never told Hal, and Hal died believing the boy was his. Tobias has never asked and I do not think he ever counted the months. Nobody in this valley knows. If it came out, Hal's people would say the land was got by a lie, and Tobias would have a claim on my son, and I would sooner burn the house.

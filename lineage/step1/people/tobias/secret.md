The mill short-weights. Every sack that comes through, I take a twentieth more than the miller's share, and I have done it for nine years, since the first bad harvest. That is where the grain I lend comes from. That is the only reason I have grain to lend at all. The weighing-house stones and mine do not agree, and nobody has ever put them side by side. If they did, there would be no seat, no mill, and probably no Tobias Crane.
